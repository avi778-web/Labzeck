PRIVACY POLICY

Platform: Labzeck Effective Date: october 7, 2026 Official Legal Document

At Labzeck, accessible from our website and mobile application services, protecting user privacy and ensuring transparent data practices is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information collected, recorded, and processed by Labzeck, as well as the choices you have regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, personalize, and optimize our digital services for all our users. The data collected falls into the following categories:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Name, email address, phone number, and any communication details provided when filling out contact forms or reaching out to customer service.
  • Log & Analytical Data: Information automatically sent by your browser/device, including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click behavior.
  • Account Data: Usernames, credentials, and preference settings created during registration on the Labzeck platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Labzeck utilizes the collected information for legitimate operational and business purposes, including:

  • Operating, maintaining, and enhancing our platform features and operational stability.
  • Improving user interface design, features, and overall user experience.
  • Analyzing traffic trends and user engagement to drive continuous improvement.
  • Communicating updates, security notices, administrative support messages, and operational alerts.
  • Preventing security breaches, detecting malicious activities, and ensuring compliance with applicable laws.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like most modern web platforms, Labzeck uses 'cookies' and similar tracking tools. Cookies store information such as user preferences and pages visited, allowing us to deliver a customized experience based on browser characteristics and usage history.

You can choose to disable or clear cookies through your browser options. However, doing so may limit access to specific features or functionality on the Labzeck website.

4. Third-Party Services & Privacy Policies

Labzeck's Privacy Policy does not extend to external websites, third-party advertisers, or linked platforms. We strongly advise users to review the independent Privacy Policies of these third-party services to understand their data collection and management procedures.

5. Data Protection & Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children's online privacy is critical. LabZeck does not knowingly collect any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has submitted personal data on our platform, please contact us immediately, and we will take swift action to permanently delete such information from our systems.

7. User Rights & Consent

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored by LabZeck. By using our website or platform services, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms and conditions.
